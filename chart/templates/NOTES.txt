Nodulz uses one replica. Upgrades include a short interruption and database migrations.
Back up your database, data volume and encryption secrets before upgrading.

Local access:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ .Release.Name }} 3001:3001

Open http://localhost:3001 and sign in with the ADMIN_USERNAME and ADMIN_PASSWORD
configured in your instance Secret. These values create the administrator only
on the first start; changing them does not reset an existing account.
Documentation: https://nodulz.com/docs/installation
